University of Mpumalanga

Terms and Conditions

  • Accuracy of Information

    All information provided in the application must be true, complete, and accurate. Falsification may lead to disqualification or withdrawal of admission.

  • Submission Deadlines

    Applications must be submitted before the published closing date. Late applications may not be considered.

  • Supporting Documents

    Applicants are required to upload all relevant supporting documents (ID, certificates, academic records, etc.) by the deadline. Missing documents can delay or disqualify your application.

  • Application Fee

    Where applicable, the non-refundable application fee must be paid. Proof of payment should be submitted with the application.

  • Admission is Not Guaranteed

    Meeting the minimum requirements does not guarantee admission. Selection is subject to availability of space and academic merit.

  • Communication

    The University will communicate application outcomes and updates via email and/or SMS. It’s the applicant’s responsibility to ensure contact details are correct.

  • Changes to Programme Choices

    Applicants may request changes to programme choices within the application period. After the closing date, no further changes will be permitted.

  • Language of Instruction

    The language of instruction at the University of Mpumalanga is English. Applicants must be proficient in English to succeed academically.

  • Data Privacy

    All personal information submitted will be used solely for the purposes of admission and will be protected under applicable privacy laws.

  • Conduct and Integrity

    Applicants are expected to act with integrity and respect the University’s values throughout the application process.
